**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the Radio-Oracle app for mobile devices, created
and maintained by OpenARDF as an open-source service. This service is provided
"AS IS".

**Information Collection and Use**

Radio-Oracle stores event-management data that you enter or import into the app,
including races, categories, competitors, SportIdent card readouts, results,
print settings, and result-service configuration. This data is stored on your
device unless you choose to export it, share it, print it, or send it to a
configured live-results service.

The app may collect limited diagnostic information to help improve reliability,
such as:

*   device and operating system information
*   app version and app state
*   crash reports and diagnostic events
*   network state needed for result-service features

Radio-Oracle does not collect precise device location.

**Third Party Access**

Radio-Oracle uses third-party services only for app diagnostics and optional app
features. These services have their own privacy policies:

*   [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
*   [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/)

If you configure a live-results provider or export/share files from the app,
the data you send is handled by the service, app, or recipient you choose.

OpenARDF may disclose user-provided or automatically collected information:

*   as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal
    process;
*   when disclosure is necessary to protect rights, safety, investigate fraud,
    or respond to a government request;
*   with trusted service providers who work on OpenARDF's behalf and are bound
    to handle the information consistently with this policy.

**Opt-Out Rights**

You can stop all collection by uninstalling the app. You can also avoid optional
sharing by not configuring live-results services and not exporting or sharing
event files.

**Data Retention Policy**

Event data stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear app
data, or uninstall the app. Diagnostic data handled by Firebase is retained
according to Google's Firebase retention practices.

For deletion requests involving data controlled by OpenARDF, contact
openardf@gmail.com.

**Children**

Radio-Oracle is not directed to children under the age of 13. OpenARDF does not
knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If
you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact
openardf@gmail.com so appropriate action can be taken.

**Security**

OpenARDF takes reasonable measures to protect information processed by the app.
No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

**Changes**

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be made by
publishing the revised policy.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-06-01.

**Your Consent**

By using Radio-Oracle, you consent to the processing of information as described
in this policy.

**Contact Us**

If you have questions about this privacy policy or privacy while using
Radio-Oracle, contact OpenARDF at openardf@gmail.com.
